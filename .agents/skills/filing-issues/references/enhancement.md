# Filing an enhancement

- **Motivation** comes from the user's side: what a deck author cannot
  express today, or what a reader of the output cannot see, and why it
  matters for the modelled sector.
- **Intended behavior** says what a deck author should be able to do, or a
  reader of the output should be able to see, once the need is met. It is
  described from the user's side, not as attributes, commands or code.
- **Considerations** is where a DSL spelling or design idea goes, if one is
  mentioned at all: a consideration, never a requirement. It also holds
  constraints, such as decks that must keep working.
- Assumption values the need depends on come with references or a
  justification.
