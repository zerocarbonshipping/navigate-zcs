# Navigate Brand Policy

## Purpose
Navigate is an open-source project maintained by Fonden Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Center for Zero Carbon Shipping (the "Center"). We encourage broad use, modification and redistribution of the project in accordance with its open-source licences.

The purpose of this Brand Policy is to ensure that users can clearly distinguish between the official Navigate project maintained by the Center and independent forks, derivative works and other third-party implementations. Any use of the Navigate name should avoid creating confusion about the origin, maintenance or endorsement of a project.

## Official Navigate
The official Navigate project is maintained and published by the Center through its official repository and release channels.

Only the Center may describe a repository, release or publication as the official version of Navigate or otherwise represent that a project is maintained, endorsed, certified or approved by the Center.

## Forks and Derivative Works
The open-source licences permit you to copy, modify and redistribute Navigate.

If you create a fork or derivative work, you may accurately describe it as:

- "based on Navigate";
- "derived from Navigate"; or
- "a fork of Navigate".

However, you should clearly identify your project as an independent work and should not present a modified version as the official Navigate project or use the Navigate name in a manner that could reasonably create confusion about its origin, maintenance or endorsement.

In particular, if your project has been materially modified, we encourage you to adopt a distinct project name while acknowledging that it is based on Navigate.

## No Endorsement
Use of the Navigate software does not imply that the Center has reviewed, endorsed, certified or approved a third-party project, implementation, publication, analysis or commercial service.

You should not state or imply any affiliation with, endorsement by or approval from the Center without the Center's prior written permission.

## Use of the Center's Name and Identity
The Center's name, abbreviations, logos and visual identity should not be used in a manner that suggests affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement where none exists.

In particular, third parties should not use the Center's identity in project names, websites, promotional material or other communications in a way that could reasonably cause users to believe that a project or service is operated, supported or approved by the Center.

## Citation
If your work uses or builds upon Navigate, we encourage you to cite the project using the citation information provided in this repository.

## Questions
If you are unsure whether a particular use of the Navigate name or references to the Center could create confusion regarding origin or endorsement, please contact the Center before publication.

## Reservation
The Center reserves the right to object to uses of the Navigate name or the Center's identity that are misleading, create confusion regarding the source or endorsement of a project, or otherwise misrepresent the relationship between a third party and the Center.
